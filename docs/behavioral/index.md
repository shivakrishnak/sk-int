# Behavioral Interview Skills

Interview-focused deep-dive into behavioral interviews, non-technical
skills assessment, and personality-based hiring. From STAR method
fundamentals through executive presence, culture fit evaluation,
Amazon Leadership Principles, psychometric theory, and staff-level
career narrative strategy. Every keyword entry follows Interview
Mastery Dictionary v1.0.

Covers the full spectrum from L0 orientation (what behavioral
interviews are and why companies use them) through L4 expert
(Amazon LPs, executive presence, culture fit deep-dives) and
META transferable thinking patterns.

**ARCHETYPE 6 - BEHAVIORAL / SOFT SKILL applies to all entries.**
No code blocks. STAR templates mandatory. 3+ story contexts per Q.
No system design or diagram sections.
