# Platform Engineering

Interview-focused deep-dive into Platform Engineering - from Internal
Developer Platforms (IDPs) and golden paths through Backstage service
catalogs, GitOps automation, Kubernetes-based platforms, Team Topologies
dynamics, platform SLOs, TCO/ROI measurement, and staff-level platform
strategy. Every keyword entry follows Interview Mastery Dictionary v1.0.

Covers the full spectrum from L0 orientation (what platform engineering
is and why it exists) through L5 architect-level strategy (platform
roadmaps and legacy-to-IDP migrations) and L6 theory (Conway's Law,
sociotechnical systems). Includes all mandatory keyword types at L3+:
anti-patterns, decision frameworks, security models, production
diagnostics, and failure modes.
